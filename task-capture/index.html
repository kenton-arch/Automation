Do one task the way you always do, and talk us through it. We turn what you show us into a plan to take it off your plate.

  1. Share your screen

    Choose Entire Screen, then Share. Allow the microphone when your browser asks.

  2. Work and talk

    Leave this tab open and work in a new one. Explain each step as if you were training a new hire.

  3. Finish and send

    Come back here, click Finish, and send us the one file it saves.

Private by design. Nothing leaves your computer until you send the file.

Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

On a Mac and the Share button does nothing?

Your Mac has to allow your browser to record the screen the first time. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Screen and System Audio Recording (called Screen Recording on older Macs). Turn it on for your browser, reopen the browser, and come back to this page.
