Task Capture

Do the task the way you always do it and talk through it out loud. This page watches your screen and saves one file you can send.

What task are you about to do?

Before you start

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This does not work on a phone or tablet.
  2. Close anything private you do not want in the screenshots.

When you click Start, your computer will ask two things

  1. Share your screen. Click the Entire Screen tab, click the picture of your screen, then click Share.
  2. Use your microphone. Click Allow while visiting the site.
On a Mac and the Share button does nothing?

The first time, your Mac has to allow your browser to record the screen. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Screen and System Audio Recording (called Screen Recording on older Macs). Turn it on for Chrome or Edge, reopen the browser, and come back to this page.

While you work

  1. Leave this tab open. Do your work in a new tab or another app.
  2. Say what you are doing and why as you go, like you are training a new hire.
  3. Come back to this tab and click Pause before anything private, like a password.

When the task is done

  1. Come back to this tab and click Stop.
  2. Check what was heard, answer three short questions, and click Save capture file.
  3. One file lands in your Downloads folder. Send that file to the person who gave you this link.

Nothing leaves this computer. The capture is saved as a file only when you click Save.
